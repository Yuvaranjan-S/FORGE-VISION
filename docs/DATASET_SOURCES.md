# Dataset Sources & Provenance Classification

FORGE-VISION categorizes all evidence and video datasets into strict, legally compliant provenance categories to ensure forensic authenticity and prevent misrepresentation during judicial evaluation.

---

## Supported Source Categories

| Source Type | Description | Forensic Classification |
|---|---|---|
| **`AUTHORIZED_DVR_EXPORT`** | Real surveillance exports obtained from lawful DVR/NVR seizures with chain-of-custody authorization. | `AUTHENTIC` |
| **`AUTHORIZED_CCTV_RECORDING`** | Official CCTV recordings lawfully provided by facility owners or law enforcement agencies. | `AUTHENTIC` |
| **`PUBLIC_RESEARCH_DATASET`** | Academic benchmark datasets (UCF Crime, VIRAT, Avenue, UCSD) registered with citation and license. | `RESEARCH_BENCHMARK` |
| **`VENDOR_SAMPLE`** | Official test streams provided by camera/DVR manufacturers (Hikvision, Dahua, CP Plus, Matrix, Uniview, Honeywell). | `VENDOR_SAMPLE` |
| **`FORENSIC_DISK_IMAGE`** | Bitstream raw or E01 disk images obtained from storage media. | `AUTHENTIC` |
| **`TEAM_COLLECTED_TEST_DATA`** | Controlled test recordings collected by forensic teams under documented consent and scenario parameters. | `TEAM_TEST_DATA` |
| **`SYNTHETIC_DEMO`** | Built-in scenario generated by deterministic algorithms. Explicitly labeled throughout. | `DEMO_ONLY` |
| **`USER_UPLOADED`** | Arbitrary user uploads during investigation. | `USER_UPLOADED` |

---

## Registration of Public Research Datasets
To respect copyright and terms of use, FORGE-VISION allows investigators and academic evaluators to **register** external benchmark datasets (such as UCF Crime or VIRAT) with complete bibliographic citations, licensing terms, and local filesystem pointers without unauthorized redistribution of raw third-party bytes.
